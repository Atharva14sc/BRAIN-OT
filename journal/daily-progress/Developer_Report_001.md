# Objective

Establish the research direction for the project by selecting an appropriate application domain, identifying a meaningful research gap, and proposing a novel cybersecurity solution suitable for Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and Operational Technology (OT) environments.

---

# Background

Modern Industrial Control Systems are becoming increasingly connected to enterprise networks and cloud infrastructure, exposing critical industrial assets to sophisticated cyber threats. Existing Intrusion Detection Systems primarily focus on attack detection and alert generation, with limited emphasis on continuously evaluating the trustworthiness of industrial devices.

This session focused on defining a project capable of addressing these limitations through adaptive trust evaluation and automated cybersecurity response.

---

# Work Completed

- Finalized the project title as **BRAIN-OT (Behavioral Risk-Aware Adaptive Industrial Network Defense Platform)**.
- Selected **Industrial Control Systems (ICS)** and **Operational Technology (OT)** cybersecurity as the primary research domain.
- Brainstormed multiple project ideas before selecting adaptive industrial cybersecurity as the final direction.
- Proposed the **Behavioral Adaptive Trust Index (BATI)** as the central research contribution.
- Identified the limitations of conventional Intrusion Detection Systems regarding continuous trust evaluation and adaptive decision-making.
- Defined the overall scope and objectives of the project.
- Planned the initial high-level architecture and major software modules.

# Key Decisions

The following architectural decisions were made during this session:

- The project will focus on cybersecurity for Industrial Control Systems rather than traditional enterprise networks.
- BATI will serve as the core intelligence responsible for evaluating behavioral trust.
- The platform will follow a modular software architecture to simplify future development and scalability.
- Automated response mechanisms will be integrated alongside intrusion detection instead of relying solely on alert generation.
